IEC 62443 ARCHITECTURE GUIDE

Defense-in-Depth Principles for Hybrid Power Plants:

L3.5 DMZ Segmentation: Never allow direct Level 4 IT connections into Level 3/2 OT networks. Force RDP/SSH through PAM Jump Hosts with MFA.
IEC 61850 Substation VLAN: GOOSE messaging operates directly on Layer 2 (Ethernet) for sub-4ms trip speed. Isolate GOOSE traffic within dedicated Substation VLANs.
Organic PV (OPV) RS485 Loops: Serial RS485 loops crossing outdoor field arrays require optical isolation gateways to prevent ground loops and serial injection attacks.
PPC Failover Security: Redundant PPC Master A/B heartbeat loops must be authenticated to prevent rogue PLC takeover or setpoint spoofing.
